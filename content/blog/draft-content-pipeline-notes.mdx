This post exists to prove that drafts are excluded from production builds.

## The checklist

- Frontmatter is validated at build time.
- Slugs come from filenames, not from copy-pasted metadata.
- A bad date fails the build instead of rendering "Invalid Date".

If you can see this in production, the filter is broken.
